Privacy Notice

Travlr Getaways is a portfolio demonstration of a travel website, not a live reservation service. This notice describes the behavior of the static demonstration rather than the separate original full-stack application.

Information entered into forms

Booking and contact forms validate information locally in your browser. The demonstration does not send those entries to a server, save them in a database, or create a booking or customer account. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into a demonstration form.

Cookies and analytics

The static site does not intentionally install advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or first-party tracking cookies. Your browser or hosting provider may handle ordinary technical information necessary to deliver the site.

Hosting and third-party links

The hosting provider may process IP addresses, request details, and other standard server logs according to its own policies. Links to external safety and travel resources lead to independently operated websites with their own privacy practices.

Changes and questions

This notice may change if the demonstration adds new features or services. For questions about the project, see the contact page; its form is illustrative and does not deliver messages.
